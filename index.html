Hermes Workspace

Hermes Workspace is a private productivity integration operated for the owner of Amversi.

It connects the owner's authorized Google services—such as Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, and Google Sheets—to a private automation environment. It is not offered as a public application or service.

Access and control

Google access is granted only by the account owner through Google's OAuth consent process. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
